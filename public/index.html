            How it works
            • Past papers: open Past paper finder, pick a subject or search (e.g. OCR computer science component 2 2019), then click tags to narrow it down by level (AS or A level), exam board, paper, year, series and question paper / mark scheme. Add & open puts it in your papers. Physics mock papers in the subject list opens the Save My Exams mocks (question paper or mark scheme).
            • Type: click any dotted line, ruled line, empty table cell or answer box and type. Text sits on the lines, shrinks if you write a lot, and the page grows if it still doesn't fit.
            • Tools: Text only types (click any answer space; it never draws). Pen only draws (mouse, trackpad, Apple Pencil or a mirrored iPad). Smart does both: tap an answer space to type, drag to draw. Laptops start on Text; iPads, and devices where an Apple Pencil has been used, start on Pen. Fingers scroll; pinch to zoom.
            • Apple Pencil: ink responds slightly to pressure, and on iPads with Pencil hover a dot shows where you'll write. Tap with two fingers to undo and three fingers to redo. Tap one finger two to four times quickly, or the Pencil three times, to swap between the pen and the eraser; once you've rubbed something out and lift the Pencil, it goes back to the pen by itself.
            • Multiple choice: tick boxes, ovals and pills are found automatically. Click or tap one to choose it (a tick, or the oval filled in); choose as many as the question needs, and click again to un-choose. Options printed as plain letters (A. 6%) get a circle round the letter you tap, one per question. Missed one? Boxes → Choice, drag over it.
            • Answer colour and style: the two menus next to the font make typed answers blue (or another colour), highlighted, bold or in a tinted box, so they stand out from the question. The exported PDF looks the same.
            • Missed answer space? With Text, click the blank spot where your answer should go: TypePDF looks there again for lines or a box, and otherwise adds a space that fits the gap.
            • Live sync: sign in on two devices (say a laptop and an iPad) and open the same paper. Typing and ink appear on the other one straight away.
            • Go to…: jump straight to any question (type 3, 4b or 4b(ii)) or page (p5); on papers in Easy / Medium / Hard sections type e1a or m3. See at a glance which questions are answered, started or flagged, and hop to the next empty answer. Tapping the page number in the corner opens it too. Tab and jumps leave the question's wording in view above the box.
            • Flags: click into an answer and press ⚑ Flag to mark it to come back to, e.g. to draw it on the iPad or because it's hard. The ⚑ button in the bar lists them.
            • Folders: make folders (and folders inside folders), drag papers onto them, star papers to keep them at the top, and make a blank new attempt of any paper. Deleted papers stay in the Bin for 30 days.
            • Perfect shapes: draw a line, arrow, circle, rectangle, triangle… and hold still for half a second: it snaps to the perfect shape. Keep moving before you lift to resize it (sizes are shown in cm).
            • Protractor and ruler (📐): true to the paper's size, so it measures printed diagrams. Drag to move; with two fingers, move, twist to turn and pinch to resize it all at once (or use ⟳ and ↔). Drag the red needle to read angles. Draw along an edge for a perfect line or arc. Compass: press the centre, drag out the radius. Best fit: select plotted points, then Best-fit line or curve.
            • Move drawings: with Select, drag a box round some drawing (mouse, finger or Pencil), then drag inside the dashed box to move it, even onto another page, or drag a corner to resize it (it keeps its shape and line thickness scales too). Delete removes it; arrow keys nudge it. With Select, scroll with two fingers.
            • Code: press </> Code beside an answer (or just start typing code and it switches itself) for monospace text with no capitals or corrections. Tab and Shift+Tab indent (several lines at once too), and Enter keeps your indentation.
            • Resize on the go: while typing in a box, drag the dots on its corners to make it bigger or smaller.
            • Two pages at once: the ▯▯ button next to zoom shows pages side by side, like a book.
            • Fix the boxes: the Boxes tool lets you add, move, resize, delete or change any answer space if one was missed. Question numbers have purple markers there: fix their label, or pick Question and drag a box over a number Go to missed.
            • ✓ Marks: after Export for AI, send the files to a marker (e.g. Claude) and import the marking it sends back (choose the file or paste it). It's drawn like a teacher's red pen: ticks, crosses, comments, marks per part, totals and your score on the front page. Hide it, move or delete marks, add your own, or Export marked PDF. Your answers aren't changed.
            • Long answers over two pages: lines that carry on at the top of the next page are one answer space: keep typing and the text flows on.
            • Export PDF keeps the original paper sharp and adds your writing on top. Export for AI gives typed answers as JSON plus a small PDF of only your handwritten work, which costs far fewer tokens to mark.
            • Shortcuts: ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo · ⇧⌘/Ctrl+Z redo · ⌘/Ctrl + and − or pinch to zoom · hold Shift while drawing for a straight line · Tab glides to the next answer space, multiple-choice question or question you answer on the paper (Space chooses an option) · Shift+Tab jumps back · double-click a word, triple-click a paragraph · Esc leaves a box · G or ⌘/Ctrl+G go to a question · N next empty answer.

            2. In Claude (claude.ai or the app) open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector. Name it TypePDF, paste the link, and add it.
