TypePDF

Answer any PDF paper: type on the answer lines, write with a pen, export a filled-in PDF.

Signed out, everything stays on this device and the PDF is never uploaded. Signed in, your papers are kept in your account and sync live between your devices.

Tip Tab glides to the next question, so you never miss one. Shift+Tab jumps back.

      How it works
      • Type: click any dotted line, ruled line, empty table cell or answer box and type. Text sits on the lines, shrinks if you write a lot, and the page grows if it still doesn't fit.
      • Tools: Text only types (click any answer space; it never draws). Pen only draws (mouse, trackpad, Apple Pencil or a mirrored iPad). Smart does both: tap an answer space to type, drag to draw. Laptops start on Text; iPads, and devices where an Apple Pencil has been used, start on Pen. Fingers scroll; pinch to zoom.
      • Apple Pencil: ink responds slightly to pressure, and on iPads with Pencil hover a dot shows where you'll write. Tap with two fingers to undo, three fingers to redo.
      • Live sync: sign in on two devices (say a laptop and an iPad) and open the same paper. Typing and ink appear on the other one straight away.
      • Flags: click into an answer and press ⚑ Flag to mark it to come back to, e.g. to draw it on the iPad or because it's hard. The ⚑ button in the bar lists them.
      • Folders: make folders (and folders inside folders), drag papers onto them, star papers to keep them at the top, and make a blank new attempt of any paper. Deleted papers stay in the Bin for 30 days.
      • Resize on the go: while typing in a box, drag the dots on its corners to make it bigger or smaller.
      • Two pages at once: the ▯▯ button next to zoom shows pages side by side, like a book.
      • Fix the boxes: the Boxes tool lets you add, move, resize, delete or change any answer space if one was missed.
      • Export PDF keeps the original paper sharp and adds your writing on top. Export for AI gives typed answers as JSON plus a small PDF of only your handwritten work, which costs far fewer tokens to mark.
      • Shortcuts: ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo · ⇧⌘/Ctrl+Z redo · ⌘/Ctrl + and − or pinch to zoom · hold Shift while drawing for a straight line · Tab glides to the next answer space · Shift+Tab jumps back · double-click a word, triple-click a paragraph · Esc leaves a box.

      Sign in

      3–24 letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores. At least 8 characters for the password. There's no email, so keep your password somewhere safe. The site's admin can see your papers' names and how much space they use.

      Delete account

      Removes your account and every paper stored in it. Papers saved on this device stay here.

      Export for AI marking

      Typed answers go in a small JSON file. Only the parts you wrote or drew by hand go in a PDF, cropped to just those areas and in greyscale. Together they cost far fewer tokens than the whole paper.

      Paste a PDF

      Your browser asked for the clipboard another way. Tap the box, then choose Paste (or press ⌘/Ctrl+V). A copied PDF file or a link to one both work.

      Admin

      You're the admin because yours was the first account. You have no storage limit. Everyone shares your Cloudflare account's free 5 GB, which your other Workers also use.
